Open your Easy Vault, without the app

Your photos are never trapped in our app. With an exported vault and your recovery key, this page opens everything on your computer.

Nothing leaves your computer. Your vault is unlocked right here, in this browser tab. Nothing is uploaded, and this page isn't allowed to send anything. To be extra sure, turn off Wi-Fi before you start.

1. Export your vault from the app

In Easy Vault, go to Settings → Export encrypted vault and save the file to your computer. It's still locked: nobody can open it without your recovery key.

2. Unzip it

Double-click the file on a Mac. On Windows, right-click it and choose Extract All. You'll get a folder.

3. Open it here

Choose the folder you just unzipped. This needs a computer: phones can't choose folders.

The 25-character key Easy Vault showed you when you set up your vault or backup.

Click anything to save it, or save everything at once. Albums become folders.

This page is part of the open-source Easy Vault core. You can save it and open it from your own computer, even if easyvault.app ever goes away.
